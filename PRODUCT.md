# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students in Nagaland exploring career and education options before or alongside counselling. On the careers catalogue, they browse roles by field or search for a role or skill.

## Product Purpose

CareerBridge helps students understand themselves and explore career fields, education pathways, courses, institutions, and opportunities. Success means students can understand options and make informed choices while retaining ownership of their decisions.

## Positioning

CareerBridge is a student-focused guide for career and education exploration in Nagaland. Its principle is “Guide, don't decide.”

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public careers catalogue provides keyword search, field filtering, career summaries, and links to detailed career profiles.
- Career profiles connect careers to relevant skills, subjects, study routes, courses, examinations, and related careers where catalogue data is available.
- Public exploration is distinct from personal counselling and post-counselling guidance.
- Career information is a starting point; requirements and opportunities vary and should be confirmed with providers.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: CareerBridge.
- The user-provided palette is Deep Forest `#09332A`, Soft Ivory `#F7F8F5`, Pale Sage `#DDE4DF`, Near Black Green `#17201B`, Muted Green `#6F887B`, and Warm Gold `#F2CF78`.
- CareerBridge should guide without making decisions for students.

## Evidence on Hand

- Career, career-field, and career-skill catalogue data are implemented in `src/data/fields.ts` and `src/services/catalog.ts`.
- Existing career profile routes are implemented under `src/app/careers/[slug]/`.
- No customer testimonials or outcome statistics are established as product evidence.

## Product Principles

- Preserve student agency; present options rather than prescribe decisions.
- Make broad exploration possible before asking for personal guidance information.
- Connect career information to practical study routes and learning options when the data supports it.
- Be clear about information limits and encourage confirmation with official providers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Preserve responsive web use, semantic links and controls, keyboard focus visibility, and reduced-motion behavior in interactive catalogue experiences.
